Add a separator and it appears at the right end of your taskbar.

Moving and keeping separators
  1. Move: drag a separator along the taskbar like any app button.
  2. Keep it after restarts: right-click it in the taskbar and choose Pin to taskbar. A pinned separator stays where you put it even when this app isn't running.
  3. Once pinned, you can switch off Live below so the app stops showing a running indicator under it. To get rid of a pinned one, right-click it and choose Unpin from taskbar.
  4. Changing the look of a pinned separator updates the live button right away; the pinned copy picks up the new look after you unpin and re-pin it.
Closing this window keeps separators running. Quit from the tray icon.
